# Tango and Nib

## Tango

- Blue-charcoal cat with an angular body, one nicked ear, a longer muzzle, and big paws.
- Expressive brows and a wide range of reactions.
- Often carries the main action: carrying, pointing, fumbling, blocking, or operating a prop.
- Temperament: clever, slightly clumsy, proud when he thinks he has won, embarrassed when Nib outsmarts him.

## Nib

- Small mouse with a triangular head, asymmetrical ears, quick hands, and a compact body.
- Nimble and alert, with very fast emotional shifts.
- Often reveals the trick, the shortcut, or the hidden mechanism.
- Temperament: mischievous, quick-thinking, and happy to outmaneuver Tango.

## Duo Rules

- Keep both characters original and franchise-safe.
- Do not copy any exact Tom-and-Jerry silhouette, costume, pose, or camera angle.
- Avoid the classic blue-gray cat versus tiny brown mouse face-off framing.
- Avoid white gloves, red neck scarves, or other recognizable slapstick franchise signals.
- Use classic slapstick energy, but invent fresh expressions and fresh gags.
- Let one character drive the scene while the other reacts or complicates it.
- Do not turn them into mascots or decorative stickers. They must do the work.

## Expression Set

- Excited
- Confused
- Sneaky
- Frustrated
- Proud
- Shocked
- Relieved
- Triumphant
