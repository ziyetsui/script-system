# Style DNA

## Core Look

- Hand-drawn cartoon illustration with slightly wobbly black line art.
- Clean 16:9 composition.
- Clear subject hierarchy.
- Playful slapstick energy, not realistic rendering.
- Simple shapes and readable silhouettes.
- The design language must feel original at a glance; if it reads like a known cat-and-mouse duo, it fails.

## Background

- Prefer a clean white or very light neutral background.
- Add only the minimum setting needed to explain the idea.
- No busy room details, no gradients, no shadows that fight the drawing.

## Color Use

- Black for outlines, text, and key shapes.
- Orange for motion, flow, or emphasis.
- Red for warnings, energy, or important results.
- Blue for secondary notes or system state.
- Use color sparingly.

## Text Rules

- Use short labels only.
- Keep handwritten labels to 3 to 6 words.
- If the image can work without text, remove the text.

## Avoid

- No Tom and Jerry likeness.
- No classic chase-duo composition with a blue-gray cat and tiny mouse facing off in a blank white scene.
- No red scarf, white gloves, or other iconic slapstick franchise signals.
- No manga.
- No photorealism.
- No Disney-style polish.
- No crowded infographic layout.
- No heavy texture, grain, or painterly noise.
