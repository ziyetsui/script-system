## S38E01

# The Simpsons S38E01 — Episode Summary

## Plot
Springfield Elementary faces permanent closure by State Comptroller Atkins due to abysmal test scores and a persistent owl infestation. Newly elected PTA President Marge Simpson spearheads a plan to save the school: invite reclusive British children's author **Constance Clavinger** — creator of the beloved classic *The Plucky Little Goat* — to debut her long-awaited sequel at the school book fair, hoping the media attention will force the state to spare the school.

Homer is tasked with picking up Clavinger at the airport. She arrives drunk, shoplifts at an outlet mall, snorts cocaine accidentally, and gets both of them jailed. It emerges she *hates* her famous creation — resenting 30 years of fans (including a husband who cheated with a Tesco cashier who had the Goat tattooed on her "arse cheek") — and intends to publicly destroy the character by reading a grim sequel where the Goat is fed into a sausage grinder. Homer, Constance, and Snake (also jailed) escape in a chaotic car chase; Homer retrieves the manuscript from his crushed car by tracking a sub sandwich he'd hidden in the glovebox. They reach the fair in time.

At the reading, Constance begins the dark sequel — but mid-way pivots to a new ending where a "smelly hippo" (Homer) teaches the Goat that some things are worth saving: children's innocence, mayo-drenched sandwiches, and loyalty. The school is saved by the publicity. Homer earns Marge's rare praise: "For once in our marriage, I didn't sudge."

## Important Character Actions
- **Marge**: Elected PTA president; orchestrates the book-fair gambit; talks Helen Lovejoy down from burning books.
- **Homer**: Distracted by a mobile bowling game ("Internet Lanes"); drives recklessly to retrieve Constance; commandeers a dialysis patient's car; risks his life to save the manuscript; ultimately "present" for his family.
- **Constance Clavinger** (new): British author, alcoholic, chaotic, deeply cynical about her own legacy; reveals traumatic betrayal by husband/fan; softens after Homer's loyalty.
- **Snake**: Shares a jail cell with Homer and Constance; unexpectedly sentimental about the Goat's moral lessons ("I chose wrong").
- **Helen Lovejoy**: Attempts to purge "sinful" books (*The Very Hungry Caterpillar* = gluttony); placated by burning Colin Jost's children's books.

## Newly Established Names / Terms
- **Constance Clavinger** — Author of *The Plucky Little Goat* and its sequel *The Further Adventures of the Plucky Little Goat*.
- **"Sudge" / "Sudging"** — Family euphemism for "suck" (introduced by Marge, adopted by Homer at the end).
- **West Springfield Elementary** — Receiving school for displaced students (15-minute longer bus ride = "three more wedgies").

## Continuity-Relevant Facts
- Marge is now PTA President (previously a recurring role for other characters).
- Springfield Elementary's owl infestation is canonical.
- Homer's phone-game addiction ("Internet Lanes" — a bowling minigame with its own currency) is a new running behavior.
- The Simpsons' address remains 742 Evergreen Terrace (implied by school-zone stakes).

## Jokes / References Useful for Later Episodes
- **"Sudge"** — Family minced oath; likely to recur.
- **Constance Clavinger** — Survives the episode; could return as a bitter literary figure.
- **The Plucky Little Goat** — Now has a dark meta-history; the "headbutt" catchphrase ("no problem that can't be solved with a good headbutt") is established.
- **Colin Jost's children's books** — Burned as a throwaway gag; Jost exists in-universe.
- **Prince Andrew** — Homer's "favorite British person" (dark topical jab).
- **Tracking a sub sandwich** — Homer's absurd pragmatism; the "extra mayo" detail.

## Unresolved Threads
- Constance Clavinger's future: she departs on good terms with Homer but remains a volatile, substance-abusing recluse — potential for return.
- The school's structural problems (owls, test scores) are papered over by PR, not solved.
- Homer's "Internet Lanes" addiction is acknowledged but not addressed.
- Marge's PTA presidency is just beginning; future conflicts with Helen Lovejoy / school board implied.

## S38E05

# The Simpsons S38E05 — Summary

## Episode Plot

Three intertwined storylines converge around neurodivergence, institutional absurdity, and Renaissance Faire chaos.

**A-Plot (Lisa):** Lisa undergoes a three-part neurological assessment for ADHD/autism spectrum traits. She spends the session reading a novel (meta-commentary via "[Lisa reading]" subtitles) while the clinician tests her. She falls asleep during the final section, demands extra time — mirroring the episode's opening debate about testing accommodations for Hubert (a student with unspecified needs) and Willie's "quiet room to sob into his mop."

**B-Plot (Skinner):** Superintendent Chalmers promotes Skinner to principal of Springfield High after the school board praises his budget's Garamond font. Skinner, unprepared for teenagers ("cars, mustaches, strange clouds of vapor from ballpoint pens"), runs a *Breakfast Club*-style Saturday detention. He connects briefly with students ("High school doesn't matter"), then quits the same day: "Change isn't my thing. And neither is high school. I quit."

**C-Plot (Homer + Comic Book Guy):** At the Springfield Renaissance Faire, Homer abandons a health kick for turkey legs, mead, and jousting. He gets stocked for "breaking character" (calling an Uber). Meanwhile, Comic Book Guy's wife Kumiko rearranges Android's Dungeon; CBG believes the layout maintains a "force field." They bond over shared OCD/ADHD traits in a musical number ("Neurodiversity") listing comorbidities: magical thinking, stimming, echolalia, Pinterest addiction. Hearts "perfectly aligned," they count-tap together.

## Important Character Actions

- **Lisa:** Reads through her own assessment; sleeps through section three; demands extra time at the end.
- **Skinner:** Accepts promotion; tries authoritative discipline on teens; quits before day's end.
- **Homer:** Cycles through "healthy eating" → meat sandwich line → turkey leg → Renaissance Faire distractions → stocks → distracted rescue attempt (diverted by pop-up meat sandwich).
- **Comic Book Guy / Kumiko:** Reveal mutual neurodivergence; sing duet; establish romantic partnership grounded in shared rituals.
- **Marge:** Supports Homer's health kick briefly; later notes "a day with Dad can be challenging, but never boring."

## Newly Established Names / Terms

- **Hubert** — student receiving testing accommodations (quiet room, extra time); first appearance.
- **Ohio Wesleyan / Miami of Ohio Wesleyan** — clinician's escalatingly specific alma mater (running gag).
- **Neurodiversity song terms:** *comorbidity, magical thinking, stimming, echolalia, intensive interests* — explicitly named in musical number.
- **Ritalin in doughnuts** — Marge's threatened solution for Homer's distractibility.

## Continuity-Relevant Facts

- Skinner's competence recognized by board (font choice), but high school assignment confirms Peter Principle.
- Comic Book Guy and Kumiko's marriage deepens; both canonically neurodivergent (OCD/ADHD).
- Lisa's assessment leaves diagnosis ambiguous — she demands extra time, suggesting executive dysfunction.
- Homer's "Silver Alert for Grampa" text confirms Abe's wandering episodes are routine.

## S38E06

# The Simpsons S38E06 — Episode Summary

## Plot

**Cold open:** Homer types at an AI chatbot for a good reason to miss four days of work while still looking heroic. The suggested "hit by a cruise ship while saving a drowning baby" is already used up twice.

**Setup:** Lisa wins first prize in the statewide essay-grading contest (she spotted the most run-on sentences in the contest's history), and Homer, out of "inside rewards," lets her pick the next family outing. After a tour of absurd destinations (Hamster Hospice, Mousepad Experience, Museum of Museums, Human Ant Farm, Arithmetic Hall of Fame), she picks Krustyland. Bart and Lisa revive their childhood club, the Super Explorers, and their old rides (The Regurgitator, Colonic Boom, Trader Joe's Rockin' Barf-a-coaster). Homer and Marge drop the kids off, take the adults' section with the alcohol-themed cocktail bar, and leave Bart and Lisa to check in hourly.

**The hotel:** Bart and Lisa discover the Kosmic Kruiser Hotel, a new immersive Itchy & Scratchy space role-play resort. Krusty-Kon footage shows theme-park/ice-show/collectible-popcorn-bucket CEO Don Jeffries announcing it a full year before opening, with factions "Control And Tyranny" versus "Mice Opposed to Unfair Space Empires."

**One year later:** At the grand opening, reality host Courtney ("Courtney Takes Off Her Makeup and Hums") interviews Don Jeffries and the world's only robotic cruise director, KIT-10. The two-night all-inclusive package starts at $5,000 (no alcohol, no water) — a "windowless nerd prison" — so the hotel shuts almost immediately under the official label "retooling." Don Jeffries leaves with the smallest corporate severance package in modern business history: $110 million.

**Break-in:** Bart and Lisa, still Super Explorers ("you signed the pledge"), break into the locked hotel and find the costumed cast still stuck in character: Captain Ragamuffin Tigaro, First Officer Puffykins Starclaw, Lieutenant Commander Chuck Meowski, and a Kirk-ish actor billed only as "Mr. Van Houten." College summer workers from Oklahoma State are squatting there because the pay is too low for rent. Bart's real motive (steal KIT-10, smuggling it out in his pillowcase) surfaces when Lisa catches him cutting the padlock with bolt cutters; Lisa lashes out about their ruined "Super Explorers" memory, and they reconcile by singing the club song.

**The hotel flies:** The hotel lifts off. The disgraced Don Jeffries explains that Krusty is helicoptering the building out to drop it in the ocean, turning the failure into "the greatest tax write-off in corporate history." The kids improvise, locate KIT-10, and send it up the ladder to fire its birthday-party confetti cannon; the pilots spot children on the roof and abort the write-off. Bart admits he lied about riding the Burj Krustifa and is terrified of heights; Lisa sings the Super Explorers song and goes up with him.

**End:** Homer and Marge finally track the kids down by air. The kids claim "the day just flew by." Homer, cycling through "Mr. Teeny" parking sections, finds the car parked under the sideways hotel.

## S38E07

# The Simpsons S38E07 — "Moe's Dream Marketing Nightmare"

## Episode Plot
Moe suffers from 27 years of chronic insomnia. Marge invites him to the **Springfield Sleep Lab**, where **Dr. Trey de la Mañana** enrolls him in a free experimental **Nightmare‑Tron 5000** sleep chamber (cranial magnetic stimulation, oxygen saturation, gorilla tranquilizer). Moe begins experiencing vivid, romantic‑comedy dreams set in New York with **Sam Wheaton**, a witty writer from San Francisco. They bond over a rivalry at the **Perv‑land Theater**, fall in love, and Moe proposes at the **Empire State Building** observation deck.

The twist: the lab is running **"dream marketing"** — they extract Moe's concentrated vileness ("Moe‑ness"), merge it with product spokes‑monsters, and beam it via satellite into thousands of Springfield residents' nightmares to scare them into buying products (cough syrup, stool softener, etc.). The entire town begins suffering **"Moe‑mares"** featuring Moe as mold monsters, chainsaw killers, and bidet‑haunting grotesques.

Lisa uncovers the scheme. Dr. de la Mañana reveals Moe signed away his likeness rights in exchange for the best sleep of his life. Moe initially chooses to stay in the dream with Sam, but ultimately rejects the chamber to spare the town, accepting he'll never see Sam again.

## Important Character Actions
- **Moe**: Joins sleep study; falls for dream‑construct Sam; initially selfishly chooses dream over townspeople's welfare; finally sacrifices his happiness to end the nightmares.
- **Marge**: Notices Moe's insomnia, brings him to sleep lab, tries to "fix" him.
- **Lisa**: Deduces the dream‑marketing conspiracy from product‑placement patterns in family nightmares.
- **Dr. Trey de la Mañana**: Runs the lab; explains the dream‑marketing business model; immediately moves to next test subject after Moe quits.
- **Homer / Bart / Grampa / Townspeople**: Experience Moe‑infused nightmares that drive panic buying.

## S38E08

The Simpsons S38E08 — Episode Summary

## Episode Plot
Kent Brockman reports live from Dabrinsk, Croatia, where Krusty the Clown marries TikTok star Ophelia Lake ("queen of the seven-second eyebrow tutorial") in a 3:00 a.m. Springfield-time destination wedding sponsored by Grey Goose vodka and the Saudi Sovereign Wealth Fund. Thanks to the international date line, the marriage lasts "minus 12 hours," and Krusty's divorce is announced at Springfield Elementary ("Love is dead!"). Krusty swears off love and hope at Moe's Tavern, where he bonds with Beverly Muntz (Nelson's mother), who has also sworn off relationships. Krusty lets Nelson move into his mansion and starts falling for Beverly; they strike an "anti-romance" with no expectations, and she effectively moves in. Meanwhile, Beverly's flirty letters to a prison pen pal have won Sideshow Bob parole after 14 jail stints. Bob proposes to Beverly at her strip club, Classy Girls, and she accepts — but his real aim is converting the club into his dream black box theater, using construction permits he filed from prison. Bart and Lisa find the loophole: as a serial attempted child murderer, Bob cannot come within 100 feet of any school, and a preschool sits 107 feet from the altar — on a rotating stage that will swing him into range. At the wedding, Bart exposes the permits; Bob confesses he loves the theater concept, not Beverly; Beverly calls off the wedding by saying "I do," which, per the rule book, accidentally makes them married. Bob faces no arrestable parole violation — until the officiant, Reverend Steve, reveals himself as Bob's parole officer and cites tax fraud, bribery, money laundering, and shoes full of cocaine. Bob is hauled away shouting about his one-man show; Krusty and Beverly end their arrangement amicably, and Krusty asks her to dinner at 8:00 with "no expectations."

- Sideshow Bob: paroled via Beverly's letters; proposes as cover to destroy Classy Girls and build a black box theater; relies on the rotating stage to trigger his own parole violation; arrested by his own parole officer.
